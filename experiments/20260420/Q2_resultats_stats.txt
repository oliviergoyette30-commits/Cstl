Phrase paper §6 :
"Family H (intensity adverbs, n=8) shows CSTL v3.0.4 underperforming
JSON by 12.5%. The current spec lacks a dedicated operator for graded
intensity, forcing encoders to overload the polarity weights +/-/°.
This motivates v3.1, where graded conventions (° / + / ++ / +++)
extend the existing operators without introducing new glyphs."
